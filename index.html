01 / The Challenge

30%

of adults over 60 have sarcopenia — significant muscle loss that limits daily life and independence.

Sarcopenia Preventable

Why this matters

— We lose 3–5% of muscle mass per decade after age 30, accelerating after 60
— Muscle loss leads to falls, fractures, and loss of independence
— Lower muscle mass is linked to slower metabolism and chronic disease
— The good news: strength training reverses muscle loss at any age

02 / Why It Works

6 reasons to start today

01 Rebuild Muscle Mass Resistance training directly stimulates muscle protein synthesis at any age
02 Improve Balance & Stability Stronger legs and core dramatically reduce fall risk
03 Boost Bone Density Weight-bearing exercise increases bone mineral density, preventing fractures
04 Increase Metabolism Each pound of muscle burns more calories at rest, aiding weight management
05 Sharpen Mental Clarity Exercise increases blood flow to the brain and boosts mood-enhancing hormones
06 Enhance Independence Functional strength means carrying groceries, climbing stairs, and living freely

05 / Before You Begin

Four rules. No exceptions.

01 Consult Your Doctor Especially important if you have heart conditions, joint problems, or haven't exercised in over a year. Get medical clearance first.
02 Warm Up Every Session 5–10 minutes of light movement before every workout. Cold muscles tear. Warm muscles grow.
03 Form Over Weight Never sacrifice technique for heavier loads. Poor form causes injury; injury stops progress. Start light, always.
04 Listen to Your Body Sharp pain means stop. Discomfort is normal; pain is a signal. Rest when needed — it's part of the program.

10 / Exercise Guide

The six movements that matter most

SQUAT Goblet / Dumbbell Squat Hold weight at chest. Feet shoulder-width apart. Lower until thighs parallel. Drive through heels to stand.
PUSH Push-Up (Wall → Modified → Full) Straight body line from head to heels. Lower chest to floor. Elbows at 45° — not flared wide.
PULL Dumbbell Row Hinge at hips, back flat. Drive elbow toward hip — not up and back. Squeeze shoulder blade at top.
PRESS Overhead Press (Seated) Sit tall, core tight. Press dumbbells overhead to full extension. Lower with control to shoulder height.
CORE Bird-Dog On hands and knees, extend opposite arm and leg. Hold 3 seconds. Keep hips level throughout.
HINGE Step-Up Place full foot on step. Drive through front heel to stand. Full hip extension at top. Step down with control.

12 / Nutrition

Fuel your
muscle growth

Daily protein target

1.4g

per kg of body weight per day. A 180 lb (82 kg) man needs approximately 115g protein daily.

Daily principles

—Protein at every meal — aim for 25–35g per sitting
—8+ glasses of water daily — more on training days
—Anti-inflammatory foods — fatty fish, leafy greens, berries, olive oil
—Don't skip breakfast — it sets up protein intake for the day
—Post-workout protein within 45 min — the anabolic window is real

13 / Recovery & Rest

Muscle is built during rest, not during training

Zzz Sleep 7–9 Hours Growth hormone is released primarily during deep sleep. Poor sleep = poor recovery = poor results. Prioritize it like a workout.
↻ Respect Rest Days At least 1–2 full rest days per week. Light walks are fine. Muscle fibres repair and strengthen on off days.
≋ Stretch After Sessions 5 minutes of gentle stretching after each session. Focus on hamstrings, chest, and shoulders — the muscles you just worked.
◎ Stay Hydrated All Day Dehydration significantly impairs muscle recovery. Aim for 3+ litres on training days. Pale urine = well hydrated.
